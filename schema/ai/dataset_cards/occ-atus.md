# American Time Use Survey (ATUS) microdata files (occ-atus)
- UBEM workflow roles: operation_use
- Statistical nature: sample_survey_weighted
- Finest public spatial granularity: county
- Temporal resolution: P1D
- Access: open_download
- Join keys: fips_state, timestamp
- License: US-Government-Public-Domain
- Survey weights: TUFINLWGT (final weight, 2006-2019 and 2021 onward); TU06FWGT for 2003-2005; TU20FWGT for 2020; 160 replicate final weights FINLWGT001-FINLWGT160 in the replicate weights files; module weights EUFINLWGT, WUFINLWGT, LUFINLWGT for Eating and Health, Well-Being and Leave modules
