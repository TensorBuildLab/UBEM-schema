# Past reviewer concerns and where the new version answers them

Concerns are paraphrased from the Urban Informatics reviews (2024) and from the editors' decisions at the other
venues. Section numbers refer to `paper/manuscript.md`.

| # | Concern (paraphrased) | Answer in the new version |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research gap asserted without evidence or citations | Gap is computed from the catalog (Sect. 4.2 to 4.3) and positioned against the closest work in Table 1 |
| 2 | Problems of open data (inconsistency, incompleteness, competing standards) not stated clearly | Facets make each problem countable: granularity, statistical nature, license, linkage (Sect. 3.2, 4) |
| 3 | Purpose of the taxonomy figure unclear | Old figure dropped; faceted taxonomy (Table 2) replaces the mixed category list |
| 4 | Web pages cited instead of papers; GIS/remote-sensing literature and OSM / Scientific Data footprint datasets missing | 145 verified references; descriptor papers for each dataset in the catalog; GIS literature in Sect. 2.1 |
| 5 | Limitations hidden in a summary; bold text; English | Gap lines end each related-work subsection; per-role indicators; full rewrite in the 2026 voice |
| 6 | Semantic schema introduced abruptly; no definition; no spatial, temporal or semantic relations | Sect. 3: requirements, DCAT/CSVW/SKOS/QUDT grounding, aggregation semantics, granularity order, concept hierarchy, mappings |
| 7 | Three case-study datasets not introduced or cited | Benchmark data defined in Sect. 5.1 with codebook statistics; catalog entries carry citations |
| 8 | Rationality and advantage of the schema not evaluated | Competency questions (Sect. 4.4) and the L0 to L3 ablation that isolates the schema's semantics (Sect. 5.3) |
| 9 | LLM uncertainty and accuracy not controlled; generalizability not discussed | Gold labels, five repeats, agreement metrics, confidence-coverage curve, open-weight models, cached outputs (Sect. 5.4) |
| 10 | (Editors) Scope unclear between review and method; outdated | Reframed as a resource-and-benchmark paper with four research questions; 2023 to 2026 datasets and literature |
